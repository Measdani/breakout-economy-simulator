How to Read This Data

Each policy reflects a complete economic configuration tested in the NAiERM model.

  • Fiscal Balance - Government surplus or deficit
  • Revenue - Total federal income under the model
  • Work Incentive - Likelihood of continued economic participation

All results are generated from real public input and deterministic simulation modeling.
